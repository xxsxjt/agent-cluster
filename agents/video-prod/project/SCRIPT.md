# 视频制作项目：《再看，就把你瞪死》预告片

**视觉**：灰暗废墟、断墙、灰狼扑来、苏铭瞪眼、灰狼恐惧逃跑
**关键帧1-1**："A young scavenger in worn clothes, standing against a broken concrete wall in a post-apocalyptic ruined city, grey mutated wolf leaping at him, cinematic wide shot, dark moody atmosphere, realistic style"
**关键帧1-2**："Close-up of a young man's intense eyes, dark brown pupils glowing faintly with dark purple light, veins visible around eyes, staring directly at viewer, cinematic lighting, ultra detailed"

### Scene 2：铁壁城 (0:35-1:10)  
**视觉**：巍峨城墙、城门、擂台竞技场、苏铭对战秦昊

### Scene 3：幽界入侵 (1:10-1:45)
**视觉**：火光冲天、幽界人军队、城墙防御战
**关键帧3-1**："An army of grey-skinned humanoid monsters with shell armor and curved blades charging across a burning wasteland, dark purple energy aura, night battle, firelight, cinematic war scene"

### Scene 4：种子与根系 (1:45-2:20)

**关键帧4-2**："Underground cave system, dark purple spiral cracks in walls expanding like roots, glowing mineral veins spreading like tree roots, bioluminescent, otherworldly"

### Scene 5：主根对峙 (2:20-2:55)
